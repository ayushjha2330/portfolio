I build data-driven Meta Ad campaigns that generate high-quality leads, drive e-commerce sales, and maximize your Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

Stop wasting money on boosted posts. I handle the entire ad pipeline: installing the Meta Pixel for accurate tracking, writing persuasive ad copy, designing eye-catching creatives, testing multiple audience variations, and continuously scaling winning ads across Facebook and Instagram.

Capabilities

Ad Services Included

Campaign Strategy & Setup

Structuring the Meta Business Manager, defining clear funnel objectives (Lead Gen, Conversions, Traffic), and organizing campaigns for optimal budget utilization.

Advanced Audience Targeting

Building highly targeted core audiences, custom retargeting lists (website visitors, engaged users), and Lookalike audiences (LAL) to reach your ideal buyers.

Ad Creative & Copywriting

Designing thumb-stopping image and video creatives, paired with persuasive, direct-response copywriting and strong hooks that drive clicks and conversions.

Pixel & Tracking Integration

Installing the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI) on your website to ensure accurate data tracking, event measurement, and algorithm optimization.

A/B Split Testing

Continuously testing different headlines, creatives, primary texts, and audience variations to identify what delivers the lowest Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

Scaling & Reporting

Monitoring daily ad spend, cutting underperforming ad sets, scaling winners vertically/horizontally, and providing detailed performance reports.

Workflow

How I Scale Ads

01

Research & Strategy

Analyzing your offer, ideal customer persona, competitors, and defining the primary campaign objective.

02

Technical Setup

Configuring Business Manager, Domains, Meta Pixel, and setting up custom conversion events.

03

Creative Production

Writing multiple ad copy variations and designing the initial batch of image and video creatives.

04

Testing Phase

Launching initial ad sets with smaller budgets to test audiences and creatives in a controlled environment.

05

Optimization

Analyzing the early data, pausing poor performers, and refining the target audiences and copy.

06

Scaling Winners

Increasing budgets on high-ROI ads and building lookalike audiences based on the new conversion data.
