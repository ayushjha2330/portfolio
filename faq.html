General

Common Questions

Most frequently asked questions
about my services.

What services do you offer?

I offer a full range of digital services including Web Development (MERN Stack, Next.js, WordPress), Search Engine Optimization (On-page, Technical, Local SEO), Graphic Design (logos, social media creatives, branding), Video Editing, and Meta Ads management. Each service is tailored to your specific business needs.

How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on the complexity and requirements of your project. A simple landing page starts from ₹5,000, while a full-featured web application can range from ₹15,000 to ₹50,000+. I provide a free consultation and quote after understanding your needs.

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website typically takes 5-7 days. More complex web applications or e-commerce sites can take 2-4 weeks depending on the features required. I always provide a timeline estimate before starting any project.

Do you provide hosting and domain services?

Yes, I can help you set up hosting and domain registration. I recommend reliable hosting providers and can handle the entire technical setup for you. Hosting costs are separate from development fees.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Absolutely! I offer monthly maintenance packages that include content updates, security patches, performance optimization, backups, and technical support. This ensures your website stays fast, secure, and up-to-date.

Process

How It Works

Most frequently asked
questions about process.

What is your working process?

My process is simple: 1) Discovery Call — I understand your requirements and goals. 2) Proposal — I provide a detailed plan and quote. 3) Design & Development — I build your project with regular updates. 4) Review & Revisions — You review and I refine. 5) Launch — We go live and I provide ongoing support.

How do I make a payment?

I accept payments via UPI, Bank Transfer, PayPal, and Razorpay. A 50% advance payment is required to start the project, with the remaining 50% due upon completion. Payment terms may vary for larger projects.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, I offer up to 3 rounds of revisions for web development projects and unlimited revisions for design work until you are satisfied. My goal is to deliver exactly what you need.

What if I need changes after the project is done?

Minor changes after delivery are covered. For major additions or new features, I provide affordable maintenance packages or quote separately. I recommend a maintenance plan to keep your site updated.

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

Not at all! I handle all the technical aspects. I communicate in simple terms, keep you updated throughout the process, and provide training on managing your website content if needed.
