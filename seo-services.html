FAQs

SEO Questions

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO is a medium to long-term strategy. Initial ranking improvements and indexation updates typically appear within 4-8 weeks, with significant organic traffic growth occurring over 3-6 months.

What is included in your Technical SEO audit?

The technical audit covers crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, site speed optimization, schema markup validation, broken links, canonicalization, and mobile usability.

Do you help with Local SEO and Google Business Profile?

Yes, I help optimize your Google Business Profile, implement local business schema markup, and target location-based keywords to rank higher in local searches.
