FAQ

Course
Questions

Common questions about the MERN Stack course answered.

How long is the course?

The course covers 10+ modules and 80+ lessons. At a pace of 1-2 hours daily, most students complete it in 3-4 months. You get lifetime access, so you can go at your own speed.

What is the course fee?

Fees are available on request. Reach out via the contact form or WhatsApp for current pricing and any batch discounts.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No prior experience is needed. The course starts from absolute basics — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — before progressing to the full MERN stack. A computer with internet access is all you need.

Is the course online or offline?

The course is conducted online via live sessions and recorded videos. You can join from anywhere in India or internationally at your own convenience.

Do I get a certificate on completion?

Yes, students who complete the course and build the required projects receive a certificate of completion, which you can share on LinkedIn and your portfolio.
